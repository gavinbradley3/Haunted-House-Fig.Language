The Lady in Green — new sounds

Every sound new in Phase 11, exactly as it is in the game files (src/assets/audio/). In the game each one is also placed in 3D (it comes from a direction and distance) and passes through the room's echo, so it sounds further away and softer than here. Use headphones or the classroom speakers you will use. Reply "approved", or name any clip to drop or replace.

Her laugh (the signature sound)

Soft and closed-mouthed, with a faint echo, heard far off in the house. Rare: at most once every five minutes, and sometimes just after a painting changes or after she is seen.

giggle-1.mp3
The main laugh: a calm, slightly creepy chuckle.
giggle-2.mp3
The same woman, a shorter laugh.
giggle-3.mp3
The same woman, the shortest and quietest.

Her whisper

The same performer whispering in several layers, so no words can be made out. Heard close behind one shoulder, or through a wall. whisper-4 is reversed, for variety.

whisper-1.mp3
whisper-2.mp3
whisper-3.mp3
whisper-4.mp3
Reversed.

Her sigh

The same performer. Played as a mirror in the Hall of Mirrors fogs over, as if someone breathed on the glass.

sigh-1.mp3

Her humming

A few bars of a dark lullaby, muffled, from behind a wall, while a student lingers in a room. A different performer from the laugh and whisper; muffled and distant enough that this should not show. Drop it if you disagree.

hum-1.mp3
hum-2.mp3

Knocks in the wall

Single knuckle knocks on a wooden door, muffled as if through a wall. The game spaces them itself (three slow knocks; sometimes one more, later). They replace the synthesised knocks.
