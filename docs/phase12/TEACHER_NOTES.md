# The Last Dance: teacher notes

A bonus mystery of about 10–12 minutes that begins after the ninth lesson and the Lady's letter. It is optional: a student who never plays it has still finished the game, and the end card says so.

## What happens (spoilers)

**During the lessons** (each happens once, never during a lesson or a writing check):

- After the Hall of Mirrors lesson, walking back up the corridor, the student sees a woman in green at the far end, she steps out of sight, and the door behind them slams. *"Someone else is in the house."*
- After the fourth lesson, coming back into the entrance hall, a woman hums by the fireplace and the Lady's portrait is empty. When the student looks away, she is back.

**After the lessons:**

1. The chapter card: *The Last Dance — discover what happened to the missing guests.* A music box plays upstairs and stops; three knocks come from behind the locked doors.
2. The Lady's diary on the upper landing now holds a loose page with four clues.
3. The clues match four paintings. The student sets them on the picture lock of the right-hand door upstairs.
4. The Guest Records room: the guest book shows nine guests who signed in and never signed out, and which portrait each was painted into. Her key falls from the book.
5. Her key opens the left-hand door: the Lady's own room.
6. Looking into her mirror sets off the final encounter (below).
7. Her last letter, on her desk. A closing card. On the way out, someone is standing in the empty frame by her door.

## The lock's answers

| Clue | Painting | Where it hangs |
|---|---|---|
| I. The threshold she could not cross. | *Cold Feet* | The long corridor |
| II. The silence after the final dance. | *A Deafening Silence* | The Portrait Gallery |
| III. The reflection where no mirror stood. | *Still Life with a Pewter Jug* | The Portrait Gallery |
| IV. The single illuminated window. | *The Dolls' House at Night* | The Nursery |

Help built in: a hint button on the clue page and on the lock; after two wrong tries the hints appear by themselves, and after three the wheels that are already right glow gold. A wrong try never resets anything. **J** rereads the clues from anywhere in the house. Paintings the student has examined are marked "seen" on the lock.

## The final encounter

The camera is held on the Lady's mirror for about 13 seconds: the music box plays, stops dead, the room goes quiet, three knocks come on the door behind, the door slams, and she appears in the mirror, standing just behind the student. When the student turns round, nobody is there. One sharp sound (the "sting") marks her appearance; it is the loudest moment in the game, but kept below painful levels.

**Gentle** (Menu → "How active the house is" → Gentle): the same story, told softly. The door swings shut instead of slamming, there is no sting, and she fades in slowly. Students who find the game frightening should use Gentle. With strange events **Off**, the two moments during the lessons do not happen; the bonus chapter still plays, in its Gentle form. Nothing flashes, and the camera never moves on its own except to hold still on the mirror.

## Discussion question

The ending leaves one question open on purpose: **was the Lady trying to save her guests, or did she trap them?** Her last letter gives evidence both ways ("I thought I could keep them safe" / "I could not bear to let them go"). It works well as an exit ticket: one sentence of opinion and one quotation as evidence.

## Saving

Everything is saved as the student goes, like the lessons. A student who closes the tab mid-mystery carries on from the same step, in the same room. No story moment ever replays after a reload.
