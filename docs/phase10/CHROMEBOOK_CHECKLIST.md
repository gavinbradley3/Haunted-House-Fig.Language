# Real Chromebook checklist (about 30 minutes)

Do this on **one actual school Chromebook**, signed in with a **student test account** (not a teacher account: policies differ), using the published student address. Nothing below has been checked on a real Chromebook yet; every item is still open.

Write the model and its graphics name here: ____________________ (Settings → About ChromeOS → Additional details, or open `chrome://gpu` and note "GL_RENDERER").

## 1. Starting (5 min)
- [ ] The title screen appears within about 30 seconds on the school network. Time it: ______ s.
- [ ] "Enter the House" works; the arrival plays (or "Skip the arrival" skips it).
- [ ] Sound plays after the first click; **M** mutes; the Menu volume slider works.

## 2. Controls (10 min)
- [ ] Tap/click the house: the pointer is captured and the **trackpad** turns the view smoothly. Try Menu → Look speed if it feels too fast or slow.
- [ ] **Esc** releases the pointer; pressing **Esc** again opens the Menu.
- [ ] A **two-finger swipe** on the trackpad turns the view without capturing the pointer.
- [ ] **W A S D** and the arrow keys walk; **Shift** walks faster.
- [ ] Walk to the Hall of Mirrors (follow the bottom-left line). **E** on the large mirror opens the lesson.
- [ ] In a lesson: choose an answer, "Check my answer", Next, Back, Explore all respond.
- [ ] In Prove: type a sentence (the Chromebook keyboard types normally; **E**, **M**, **T** do not trigger game actions while typing), add it, tick the writing check, complete the room.
- [ ] Take the key with **E**; unlock the Portrait Gallery door with **E**.
- [ ] **E** on a painting opens the viewer; zoom works; **Esc** closes it.
- [ ] Climb the entrance hall stairs to the landing; read the diary; walk back down.

## 3. Speed (5 min)
- [ ] Walking around the entrance hall feels smooth enough to play (the game has no frame counter: judge by feel, and note anything jerky).
- [ ] The long corridor and the Hall of Mirrors (the heaviest places) are still playable.
- [ ] After a few minutes of slow play the picture may become a little softer (the game lowers its 3D resolution by itself). Menus and text must stay sharp.
- [ ] Nothing stutters badly when entering a room for the first time.

## 4. Saving (5 min)
- [ ] Note the room you are in, then **close the tab**. Reopen the address: the title says **Continue** with the right number of rooms; Continue returns you to the same room.
- [ ] **Sign out**, sign back in, reopen: Continue still works. *(If progress is gone here, the school's policy clears browsing data at sign-out: tell the technician; saving cannot work under that policy.)*
- [ ] **Restart the Chromebook**, sign in, reopen: Continue still works.
- [ ] Open the game in **two tabs**: the older tab shows "The house is open in another tab".
- [ ] The title screen's bottom line says progress saves on this Chromebook. It must **not** say the window cannot save (that would mean storage is blocked by policy).

## 5. Classroom conditions (5 min)
- [ ] With the sound **muted**, walk around for 3 minutes: captions in square brackets appear for the house's noises.
- [ ] With headphones on, the house's noises are noticeable but not startling. Menu → "How active the house is: Gentle" makes them rarer.
- [ ] The lesson panel text is readable at arm's length on this screen.
- [ ] Teacher edition address on the same Chromebook: it offers Continue (it sees the student's save) and **T** opens the teacher panel.
